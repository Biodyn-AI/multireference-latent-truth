### Interpretation

Every stored MAP optimum is a gradient-converged strict local maximum (largest polished max|gradient| 7.3e-10, smallest Hessian eigenvalue 0.104). Family-HB is nevertheless multimodal: the restarts split into several optima (Global 2; Immune 2; Kidney 3; Lung 4); in the global and immune fits the runner-up approximately exchanges the weights of the true class and the literature burst (runner-up w_T Global 0.0038; Immune 0.0088 vs best-optimum literature-burst weight Global 0.0038; Immune 0.0088), with credibilities that still correlate (Spearman with the best optimum Global 0.76; Immune 0.78; Kidney 0.85; Lung 0.87) but Strong sets that do not overlap (Jaccard Global 0.000; Immune 0.000; Kidney 0.002; Lung 0.000). The gap to the runner-up is large in the global universe and small in the tissue universes (Global 108.3; Immune 7.5; Kidney 7.9; Lung 11.6 nats).

The dominant-mode NUTS fits converge for every likelihood-identified quantity (R-hat Global 1.004; Immune 1.003; Kidney 1.004; Lung 1.006; minimum bulk ESS Global 1,951; Immune 1,676; Kidney 1,921; Lung 1,710; divergences Global 0; Immune 0; Kidney 1; Lung 1). Only the global fit has sites above the flag threshold, none of them likelihood-identified (muf_a;muf_b;muf_g;zj_b;zj_g; all-site maximum R-hat Global 1.016; Immune 1.009; Kidney 1.009; Lung 1.007). Random-start NUTS does not mix between the modes (R-hat Global 1.56; Immune 1.74; Kidney 1.73; Lung 1.74), which is mode trapping rather than poor sampling. MAP and NUTS agree closely within the dominant mode (Spearman Global 1.0000; Immune 0.9996; Kidney 0.9991; Lung 0.9995; Strong-set Jaccard Global 0.998; Immune 0.973; Kidney 0.947; Lung 0.947).

Bridge sampling puts the posterior probability of the second mode at Global 1.8e-46 [1.1e-46, 3.1e-46]; Immune 4.4e-3 [2.8e-3, 6.9e-3]; Kidney 2.9e-3 [1.8e-3, 4.8e-3]; Lung 6.3e-5 [3.9e-5, 1.0e-4] (Monte Carlo intervals, which cover only the within-run error; smallest to largest estimate over the draw sets and proposals that were run: Global 1.8e-46 to 2.1e-46; Immune 4.4e-3 to 8.5e-3; Kidney 2.2e-3 to 4.1e-3; Lung 6.3e-5 to 7.3e-5; log-evidence difference Global 105.32 ± 0.27 (Monte Carlo SD); Immune 5.43 ± 0.23 (Monte Carlo SD); Kidney 5.83 ± 0.25 (Monte Carlo SD); Lung 9.67 ± 0.24 (Monte Carlo SD) nats, Laplace Global 104.55; Immune 6.46; Kidney 4.91; Lung 9.33). Mixing the second mode in at these masses leaves the tissue Strong tiers essentially unchanged (Immune 326; Kidney 176; Lung 232 edges; Jaccard Immune 0.976; Kidney 0.983; Lung 1.000), but the tiers sit on a cliff: at a second-mode mass of 0.05 the context Strong set would shrink to Immune 31; Kidney 36; Lung 26 edges, while the promoted set changes little (Jaccard Immune 0.97; Kidney 0.95; Lung 0.95; at 0.10: Immune 0.89; Kidney 0.88; Lung 0.82). The promoted set is therefore less sensitive than the Strong tier to the second-mode mass; both are conditional on the selected mode.

In data simulated from the fitted model with the design-faithful simulator (post hoc), the dominant optimum is the truth-aligned one with probability Global 1.00 [0.84, 1.00]; Immune 0.93 [0.86, 0.97]; Immune (truth = real second mode) 0.60 [0.42, 0.75], so how often the wrong optimum is selected depends on which real optimum generated the data (misselection rate Immune 0.07; Immune (truth = real second mode) 0.40; 'truth = real second mode' is the reverse check). Immune-design gaps between the two optima have median 4.59 [1.24, 8.32] nats; the fraction of immune-design replicates whose gap reaches the real gap of each universe is gap ≥ real global gap 0.00; gap ≥ real immune gap 0.16; gap ≥ real kidney gap 0.14; gap ≥ real lung gap 0.02, and every such replicate selected the truth (gap ≥ real immune gap 1.00 [0.81, 1.00]; gap ≥ real kidney gap 1.00 [0.78, 1.00]; gap ≥ real lung gap 1.00 [0.34, 1.00]; replicates gap ≥ real immune gap 16; gap ≥ real kidney gap 14; gap ≥ real lung gap 2). The statement that transfers to the real fits is about the size of the gap: over 300 replicates of both design-faithful simulators in both directions, the 20 wrong selections all had gaps of at most 4.81 nats, and every replicate with a gap of at least 5 nats selected the truth (189 of 189); the real gaps are Global 108.3; Immune 7.5; Kidney 7.9; Lung 11.6 nats. Replicates reaching the real immune gap, correct of all: Global 20 of 20; Global, stratum weights 20 of 20; Immune 16 of 16; Immune, stratum weights 69 of 69; Immune, truth = second mode 0 of 0; Immune, truth = second mode, stratum weights 4 of 4; the reverse check of the primary simulator has no such replicate, so these counts are silent on that case.

### Caveats

- The primary posterior (`family_hb_nuts`) is restricted to the dominant mode; tissue Strong tiers depend on the second-mode mass, whose bridge estimate exists only where per-mode NUTS was run (see `modes_bridge.csv`). Mode-mass and mode-selection analyses (posterior_modes module) are post hoc.
- The design-faithful simulator reproduces only part of the real motif- or coexpression-observed edges (Global 62%; Immune 59%), i.e. fewer informative observations than the real data, so its mode-selection probabilities are probably conservative (inference); the fixed-mask simulator (sensitivity) gives lower probabilities and the stratum-weight simulator higher ones (Global/design-faithful 1.00; Global/design-faithful, stratum weights 1.00; Global/fixed masks 0.70; Immune/design-faithful 0.93; Immune/design-faithful, stratum weights 1.00; Immune/fixed masks 0.68; Immune (truth = real second mode)/design-faithful 0.60; Immune (truth = real second mode)/design-faithful, stratum weights 0.97; Immune (truth = real second mode)/fixed masks 0.77).
- MAP scale parameters are joint-mode values in non-centred coordinates and lie outside the NUTS intervals; they are not posterior estimates.
- PPC summary |z| values carry Monte Carlo error from a small number of replicates (20); quote observed vs replicated rates, not |z|.
- The motif-burst weight is weakly identified in the tissue universes (0.33 [0.02, 0.93]; 784 informative immune edges); credibility is unaffected.
- Recovery NUTS coverage was run only with the fixed-mask simulator and is assessable only for single-mode chain sets under the module's lenient convergence rule (Immune 1.00; chain sets Immune 4); under the frozen NUTS flag rule no truth-mode chain set is assessable (Immune 0). Pooled-chain coverage is invalid because chains mix modes. Recovery by selected mode rests on few replicates (Global/truth mode 5; Immune/competing mode 2; Immune/truth mode 18).

Under single-source dropout every estimator ranks the methods almost identically (mean rank disagreement Dependence-aware 0.0166 [0.0091, 0.0252]; EM baseline 0.0084 [0.0033, 0.0149]; Family-HB 0.0165 [0.0089, 0.0255]; Fisher 0.0216 [0.0136, 0.0316]; Majority (≥2) 0.0139 [0.0084, 0.0194]; Multifactor 0.0577 [0.0387, 0.0802]; Stouffer 0.0194 [0.0108, 0.0294]; Union 0.0049 [0.0020, 0.0085]; Vote fraction 0.0072 [0.0034, 0.0115]). ### Caveats

- Stability is not accuracy: a rule that ignores evidence is perfectly stable.
- Family and lineage protocols have only a few configurations per universe; their intervals are descriptive. Configurations that leave a single family are reported as not identified for Family-HB, and the lineage comparison uses the identified configurations for every estimator.
- Size-matched contrasts, source-clustered intervals, the EM-referenced contrasts and the mode-switch decomposition were added after the first robustness results (post hoc columns and rows); the decomposition conditions on an outcome of the Family-HB fit and does not replace the prespecified rows. All intervals are unadjusted.
- Lineage-dropout latent-class optima are weakly certified: for some fits no fresh start of the independent-seed check reached the stored optimum (Immune/Multi-integrator/Dependence-aware -40.10; Immune/Multi-integrator/Multifactor -38.39; Lung/Multi-integrator/Family-HB -0.90), and the robustness table marks the cells that aggregate fits whose optimum was found only by the extended start search or the independent-seed check.

### Caveats

- The confirmatory family (Family-HB vs EM, Fisher, Stouffer, vote count, DoRothEA, CollecTRI on AP lift) was fixed after the first held-out results; nothing was pre-registered. The smallest attainable Holm p is 1.2e-3. Composition-adjusted contrasts are post hoc and descriptive.
- ENCODE is permissive (positive rate ENCODE TFBS 0.381; ENETS2 0.014; Pooled perturbation 0.097); both ChIP references are cell-line data; perturbation positives include indirect effects. Tissue universes have few assayed TFs, so their intervals are wide; with so few TF clusters, percentile and z-based bootstrap p can disagree, so tissue within-TF contrasts are descriptive.
- The status-score and unobserved-last comparisons were defined after the outcomes were seen and are mechanism checks, not competing methods. On the ChIP references the findings 'simple rules beat Family-HB' and 'missing at random fails' are one finding: Fisher and Stouffer do not integrate evidence better, they rank unobserved edges last.
- Method AP lifts over all evaluable edges mix coverage with ranking: Geneformer attention scores only part of the edges (Immune 0.36; Kidney 0.50; Lung 0.32); its all-edge AP lift (Immune 1.33; Kidney 1.17; Lung 1.23) falls to Immune 0.97; Kidney 0.95; Lung 0.97 on the edges it scores.

### Interpretation

The informative-observation extension does not work as a generative model. Its observation term is almost the whole joint likelihood (EM + observation 0.978; Family-HB + observation 0.982), the latent class becomes a target-gene attribute (share of credibility variance between target genes EM + observation 0.829; Family-HB 0.006; Family-HB + observation 0.829), and the true-class label is not anchored: Family-HB+obs puts the true class on rarely observed genes, EM+obs on frequently observed ones (w_T by fit: EM + observation 0.322 [0.321, 0.322]; Family-HB 0.00307; Family-HB + observation 0.678 [0.677, 0.678]; Family-HB+obs (composite weights) 0.00311 [0.00298, 0.00325]), each model's label-swapped optimum being the other's best (gaps EM + observation 4,688; Family-HB + observation 68,764 nats).

Report prediction does not improve (CV Global/EM + observation -1.6e-2 ± 6.2e-4 (SE); Global/Family-HB + observation -2.7e-4 ± 1.8e-4 (SE); Immune/EM + observation -4.0e-2 ± 4.0e-3 (SE); Immune/Family-HB + observation -1.0e-3 ± 5.8e-4 (SE)). On held-out data EM+obs is above random but below the label-free status score and Fisher on ENCODE (EM + observation 1.020 [1.013, 1.028]; Family-HB 0.959 [0.943, 0.975]; Family-HB + observation 0.966 [0.942, 0.995]; Fisher 1.065 [1.045, 1.085]; 3-level status 1.050 [1.035, 1.066]) and above both on pooled perturbation data (EM + observation 1.130 [1.100, 1.180]; Family-HB 0.935 [0.896, 0.982]; Family-HB + observation 1.099 [0.994, 1.239]; Fisher 1.015 [0.980, 1.063]; 3-level status 1.065 [1.021, 1.120]); Family-HB+obs ranks below random within TFs in every universe × reference cell (within-TF AUROC 0.412 to 0.499). After TF × degree stratification EM+obs carries no edge-specific signal (EM + observation 1.07; Family-HB 1.32; Fisher 1.50; 3-level status 1.13): it is a target-gene popularity score.

In the hub-bias simulation 12 of 12 Family-HB+obs fits inflate the true class (median w_T by scenario biological hubs 0.42; hub copying 0.37; null 0.50), so the degeneracy is a property of the model, not of the data set.

### Caveats

- Entirely post hoc: the extension was designed after the held-out results; Family-HB with ignorable observation remains the primary model.
- MAP with Laplace intervals only (no NUTS); Laplace intervals assume independent edges and cover one basin. Simulation fits of Family-HB+obs cover only a subset of replicates.
- The dominance of the observation term is largely a property of the data (a class-independent observation model also puts most of the joint likelihood in that term, as the informative-observation verifier showed), not by itself a symptom of failure.
- The composite-weighted fit down-weights the replicated observation term so strongly that its posterior is close to ignorable by construction; its agreement with Family-HB (Spearman 0.98, Strong-set Jaccard 0.97) is therefore expected and does not support the extension.

### Caveats

- Context validation rests on few evaluable TFs; intervals flagged few_clusters are unstable. The perturbation context result is inconclusive, not evidence against the tiers, and cell-type mismatch (K562/THP-1 cohorts) is a competing explanation.
- q_strong cannot change the promoted set under the primary rule (property of the rule, not a finding). Comparisons with EM or rank-budget rules are confounded by set size unless size-matched (`gov_sensitivity_grid.csv`, family size_matched).
- Deconcentration keeps only part of the promoted pool and its precision cost is measured by the model's own credibility; the held-out gains are not statistically resolvable. The Pielou-evenness variant is post hoc.
- Part of the ChIP enrichment passes through literature records that cite ChIP evidence.

With known truth, even the oracle posterior is hub-concentrated relative to biology (index biological hubs 2.36 [2.30, 2.42]; hub copying 2.59 [2.47, 2.70]; null 3.30 [3.20, 3.39]), because true edges of little-studied TFs are rarely reported. Against the oracle at equal set size Family-HB is close on average without hub-concentrated copying, with the sign depending on which optimum MAP selects, and promotes hubs more under copying (biological hubs -0.38 [-0.61, -0.14]; hub copying 2.64 [2.07, 3.22]; null 0.44 [0.17, 0.70]). It mitigates rather than worsens the bias relative to the conditional-independence scores (EM − Family-HB biological hubs 0.96 [0.71, 1.22]; hub copying 3.67 [3.12, 4.22]; null 1.54 [1.25, 1.82]; EM more concentrated in biological hubs 20; hub copying 20; null 20 replicates; Fisher − Family-HB biological hubs 0.81 [0.56, 1.06]; hub copying 3.26 [2.75, 3.76]; null 1.20 [0.94, 1.45]).

Family-HB's realised FDR exceeds the nominal level in every scenario, most under hub-concentrated copying (biological hubs 0.08 [0.06, 0.11]; hub copying 0.23 [0.16, 0.30]; null 0.07 [0.05, 0.08]), with false positives concentrated at top-decile TFs (biological hubs 0.42; hub copying 0.70; null 0.50).

### Caveats

- The simulation is post hoc and uses one generator whose burst structure matches Family-HB's assumptions; the copying strength is assumed, so the size of the excess is illustrative.
- Family-HB simulation results mix qualitatively different selected optima (verifier sim-M1); quote equal-size contrasts and per-group values, not only means.
- Empirical and simulated levels are not comparable (held-out positives exist only for assayed TFs); only the pattern is.

With a TF-composition-normalised criterion the hyperparameter-grid gain is small but positive (0.0022; better in 0.84 of halves): differences within that grid are tiny, not pure noise. A joint selection objective that weighs dropout robustness against the perturbation reward never selects Family-HB (share of weight points selecting each model: EM baseline 0.18; Family-regularized 0.82; share not selecting Family-HB 1.00).

### Caveats

- Grouping-stability and sensitivity-mode analyses are post hoc.
